Calculation basis

Applied by the deterministic internal corrosion engine

OutputCalculation
Long / short rateThickness loss divided by elapsed years; linear-regression rate is calculated separately.
RCAmax(latest eligible thickness − minimum required thickness, 0).
Remaining lifeRCA divided by the governing available corrosion rate.
Calculated intervalRemaining life × configured TIF, capped by the optional maximum interval.
Recommended NIDEarlier of the calculated and configured time-based dates, when available.

Review boundaries

What this demonstration does and does not claim

QualityUnreviewed and ignored readings are excluded from corrosion-rate calculations; flags are surfaced for review.
DefaultsReference-derived rates are illustrative defaults requiring engineering review, not universal standard requirements.
Method 1A complete configured TIF is required to produce a calculated interval; DSCF/PERF are not inferred.
ApprovalApproval is version-checked against the saved input snapshot. Suggested dates are not approved inspection advice.
AI roleNo language model generates these numbers; a future AI layer may explain verified calculation outputs only.
